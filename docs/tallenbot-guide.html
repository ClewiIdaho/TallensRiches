☠ TallenBot Field Manual

Your bill tracker can text you now. It is not nice about it.

What changed

Tallens Riches used to just sit there in a browser tab until you remembered it existed. Now it installs on your phone like a real app, and TallenBot sends you one notification a day about whatever bill is closest to eating you.

One a day. Not one per bill. If four things are due, you get one message about all four.

Put it on your phone

This part matters. Notifications only work once the app is on your home screen — especially on iPhone, where they will not work at all until you do this.

iPhone

  1. Open the app in Safari (not Chrome — Safari specifically).
  2. Tap the Share button, the square with the arrow coming out of it.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Open it from that new skull icon from now on, not from Safari.

Android

  1. Open the app in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install App when it offers, or use the menu → Install app.
  3. Open it from the new skull icon on your home screen.

Turn on the roasts

  1. Tap Roasts (the bell) at the top of the app.
  2. Tap Enable Roasts, then Allow when your phone asks.
  3. TallenBot immediately sends a test one so you know it worked.

The bell turns purple and the panel says Armed when it is live.

The four settings

Daily roast time
What time of day it goes off. Defaults to 9:00 AM.
Warn me this many days ahead
How much warning you get before a due date. Defaults to 3 days. Set it to 0 if you only want to hear about things due today or already late.
Savage Mode
Unlocks the meaner pool. Your call. Ricky did not write these to be kind.
Good news too
Off by default. Turn it on and TallenBot will also tell you when nothing is due, which is the only time it is ever nice to you.

Making it stop

Open Roasts and hit Pause Roasts. That is it — it shuts up immediately and keeps all your bills and settings. Hit it again whenever you want the abuse back.

One honest catch, if you are on iPhone

Apple does not let apps like this wake up on their own in the background. So on iPhone, TallenBot will reliably roast you when you open the app — including catching you up on anything you missed — but it cannot buzz you at 9 AM while your phone is in your pocket.

On Android it can, some of the time, once installed.

The skull icon sitting on your home screen is doing most of the reminding either way. That was the whole point.

Everything else still works

Bills, income, the paycheck breakdown, the paid/unpaid toggle, backups — all unchanged. Bonus: it now works with no signal at all, so you can check it in a parking garage.

Your data still lives only on your phone. Nothing gets uploaded anywhere. Use Save Backup before you switch phones or you will lose it.
